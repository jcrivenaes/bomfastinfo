Privacy Policy for rclone-hordfast

rclone-hordfast is a private OAuth client intended solely for personal use by the owner of this site (bomfast.info).

The application uses Google OAuth to allow rclone running on the owner's personal computers to access the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google user data

Google Drive data accessed through this application is used only to read, write, organize and synchronize files requested by the owner.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics or profiling.

Storage

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally by rclone on the owner's personal computers. This website does not collect or store Google Drive files, OAuth tokens or other Google account data.

Third parties

The application relies on Google OAuth, Google Drive and the open-source rclone software. No additional third-party service receives Google Drive data through this website.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to the owner of bomfast.info.
